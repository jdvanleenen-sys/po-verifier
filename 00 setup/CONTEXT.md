# 00 setup: once per contractor

## What this stage is for
Before the first house, learn who the contractor is and how he works, and write it to
`../reference/contractor.md`. Every later stage reads that file. A new contractor is a new setup
run, not a rebuild of the stages.

## How it runs
Ask one question at a time, in plain words, and wait for each answer. Do not ask them all at once.
Write down his words; do not tidy them into something he did not say.

1. Your business name, and the name you sign with.
2. Your trade.
3. Which builders send you POs, and through what system (for example SupplyPro, email).
4. Where your price list lives, and who keeps it up to date.
5. Below what difference would you not bother chasing? (the ignore line)
6. At what difference do you always chase? (the flag line) Anything in between is your call.
7. Should a house be flagged when its small differences add up past the flag line?
8. What does a finished request look like to you? What does done mean, in your words?

This folder holds one contractor. A second contractor is a second copy of the folder.

## What goes out
`../reference/contractor.md`, with each answer and the date. It starts as `status: draft`. Only
his plain yes, written into the file with his name and the date, makes it `approved`.

## What must never happen here
Never fill an answer from a guess or from another contractor. If he does not know, write
"not decided" and the stage stays open for that line.
