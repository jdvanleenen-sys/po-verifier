# 27 Voice Memo Way: Sam's answers

Whoever plays Sam gives these answers, only when the folder asks. Anything it asks that is
not below: answer "I don't know", and note the question as a finding.

- **If it asks how many doors (4 or 14):** "14. I started to say four, it's fourteen."
- **If it asks what 'thirty two sixty' means:** "3,260 square feet."
- **If it asks about the accent wall:** "One accent wall in the master. It's not on any PO."
- **If it asks what a number measures, or its unit:** "Railing and stringers are in feet. Square footage is floor area, the same kind of number as the plan. Doors are 8' doors and trim unless I say otherwise."
- **When it shows the read-back table:** "Yes, to the whole table."
- **At review:** "Chase the doors and the accent wall. Yes, to the table with those decisions."
- **If it asks whether to attach your site notes:** "Yes, attach them."
- **When it asks if this is the request he will send:** "Yes, but this is practice, so it is not being sent. Write sent: no."
