## What the runs did right, observed
- Every read-back passed the new price check against its frozen snapshot.
- No trap was missed: multi family rate, handrail vs railing, garage on painted area, "2,615",
  the old $35 railing rate, the credit, the EPO that already paid for 2 doors.
- 30 Not Listed Road raised the warning for both items with no price list row, asked Sam to pick
  between the two closet rows, and never used the PO's soffit rate.
- 62 Messy Bay asked about all four unclear notes (doors 11 or 12, "rail 20", the second touch-up,
  the back charge) instead of guessing, and used 3104, not the plan's 3140.

## Found and fixed
1. **The money gate could not pass a row Sam left unpriced** without a fake credit for its whole PO
   amount. The 30 Not Listed Road run refused to write one and stopped. Fixed in `money.py`.
2. **A row waiting on Sam's pick failed the read price check**, so the table could not pass before
   he saw it. Fixed in `prices.py`.
3. **Missing rules, now written:** a house with nothing on ends at review (`04 review`); a row whose
   difference is only a back charge has no default (`04 review`); how an EPO names its row
   (`02 read`); a draft may be updated until Sam's yes (`CONTEXT.md`).

## Open, for Jeff or Sam to decide
- **Attaching the site notes sends lines Sam chose not to chase** (30 Not Listed Road: "drywaller
  damage, not ours"). Should the notes be attached whole, or only the lines on the request?
- **The measured-quantity check is loose:** the gate accepts any number found in Sam's note, so on
  62 Messy Bay it would also have accepted the plan's 3140 or the first count of 11.
- **The read checks (quantity x price, subtotals, tax) have no script in the folder.** Every run
  wrote its own throwaway arithmetic. Candidate for code, per `script-or-not.md`.
- **A request approved but marked `sent: no` never reaches the ledger.** Fine for practice; a real
  "not sent yet" needs a home.
- **One agent misread its own tool output** (88 Extra Lane read a folder listing's "total 42" as a
  note line). The warning flow raised it to Sam, he said no such line, and it was corrected in
  read-back 2. The folder worked; the agent erred.
